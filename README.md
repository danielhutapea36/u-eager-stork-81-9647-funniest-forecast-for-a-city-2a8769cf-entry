## The line

> Today in Drizzlewick: sunny spells, a 90% chance of raining Tuesdays, and a light breeze from the north-ish.

A flat weather-bulletin frame ("sunny spells", "a 90% chance of", "a light breeze from the...") broken twice: the rain is Tuesdays, and the wind direction is north-ish. The invented city fits its absurd weather — drizzle is in its name.
